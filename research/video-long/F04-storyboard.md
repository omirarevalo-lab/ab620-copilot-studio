## Global rules for this lesson (F02 rules + layout lint, all mandatory)
- **Lint first:** `node tools/video/lint-layout.mjs F04` must show **no BLOQUEANTE** before any full render (BORDE-CORTADO, SOLAPE, VACIO, DOBLE-EXPOSICION).
- **Margins:** every subject, label, panel and chip >= 120 px from every frame edge at 1080p, **including the bottom edge** (no cabinet, desk band or floor row touching it), in every shot: wide, close-up, exam, close.
- **Text size:** >= 30 px at 1080p everywhere, measured in the shot where it is seen: world labels in wide shots and the close, reveal explanation text, proof-scene chips (e.g. status or citation chips), bubbles and tags. A label that would be < 30 px in a wide shot is hidden there and shown only in a close-up. Bubbles/chips under 30 px are removed, not shrunk.
- **No half-empty panels:** every card/window height fits its content (no blank lower half at rest); rows exist as dim placeholders with text, then light on their words; a window arrives with its content.
- **Transitions:** no empty or grey frame > 0.5 s. The next scene's first object enters while the previous one is still leaving (crossfade ≤ 0.5 s); the camera never rests on empty floor.
- **No overlap births:** nothing appears on top of another object or crosses one in flight; every moving object has a clear destination inside the current frame; previous layer fully faded out before the next one fades in on the same spot.
- **Pills:** at most 2 on screen, each in a reserved slot (top-left / bottom-centre, both >= 120 px from the edges); no pill longer than ~15 s.
- **Exam rooms:** question card **and** all three options enter **together** at s0 ("Your turn."), dim, in full-width rows (~75 % of the width, ring at right as F02); each option brightens on its own sentence. On-screen options drop the spoken "Or" and the "?". After "Decide before I answer." the fixer inserts **3.5 s of silence** (`narration.mp3` + `beats.json`); the 3-2-1 ring starts when that sentence ENDS; nothing moves or changes shade in the silence; reveal on the reveal sentence (trap red, struck, "Trap" pill; right green with check; other at 50 %). After the reveal: explanation on the right (>= 30 px) + proof mini-scene below, so no half of the frame is empty.
- **Colour code (whole lesson, fixed):** Knowledge = teal #0f7b8a (shelves, books, citation chips); invented/ungrounded = red #c4392f dashed; settings = navy UI; Fallback = grey-blue; correct = green only. Nexo = F01/F02 blue robot. No SFX.
- **Continuity:** same Contoso IT floor, same four IT people, same Nexo robot at its desk and the same Teams door as F01/F02. The F02 library shelf grows into a full **library wing** here. **No repeats:** no new-employee slots, no systems wall, no script cabinet, no workshop, no engine room.

## 4 EXAM: the HR policies (pause s7, reveal s8) — exam room
- Q (visible from s0): "Contoso keeps its HR policies in a SharePoint site, with permissions for each department. Every employee must only get answers from documents they can already open. How do they add them?"
- A "Upload copies of the documents as files" (trap) · B "Add the SharePoint site as knowledge" (right) · C "Paste the policies into the instructions".
- Why: "SharePoint uses the **user's sign-in**, so each person only sees **what they can open**. Uploaded copies **lose those permissions**."
- Proof (built with the reveal): left, the SharePoint shelf behind the badge gate: an employee's badge lights only their department's books (green). Right, an uploaded-file drawer with the same documents open to everyone (red dashed "no permissions" chip).
