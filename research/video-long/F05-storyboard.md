## Global rules for this lesson (F04 rules + F04 review fixes, all mandatory)
- **Lint first:** `node tools/video/lint-layout.mjs F05` must show **no BLOQUEANTE** before any full render (BORDE-CORTADO, SOLAPE, VACIO, DOBLE-EXPOSICION).
- **Margins:** every subject, label, panel and chip >= 120 px from every frame edge at 1080p, bottom edge included, in every shot.
- **Text size: ALL text >= 30 px at 1080p**, no exceptions: world labels, sub-labels under labels, UI rows, UI field values, slider ends, reveal text, proof chips, bubbles, tags. Measure in the shot where it is seen (wide shots and the close included). If it does not fit at 30 px, cut the text or zoom the camera; never shrink.
- **Full frames:** every resting shot fills width and height: no subject group leaning to one side, no empty third or top/bottom band > 25 % (scene 0 and the close included). Frame the camera on the group, not on the world origin. UI windows fill ~80 % of the frame (no 190 px empty bands); scale the window up rather than leaving bands.
- **Cards and windows fit their content:** no blank lower half at rest; rows exist as dim placeholders with text, then light on their words; a window arrives with its content.
- **UI matches the voice:** every switch, badge and status shows the state the narration says at that moment (a row lit to explain it never shows a contradicting switch). No highlight without a matching word.
- **Chip and label texts are exactly as written in this storyboard** (no paraphrase, no shortening).
- **Transitions:** no empty or grey frame > 0.5 s; the next scene's first object enters while the previous one leaves (crossfade ≤ 0.5 s). Nothing appears on top of another object or crosses one in flight; every moving object has a destination inside the frame.
- **Pills:** at most 2 on screen, reserved slots (top-left / bottom-centre, >= 120 px from edges); none longer than ~15 s.
- **Exam rooms:** question card **and** all three options enter **together** at s0 ("Your turn."), dim, in full-width rows (~75 % of the width, ring at right); each option brightens on its own sentence. On-screen options drop the spoken "Or" and the "?". After "Decide before I answer." the fixer inserts **3.5 s of silence**; the 3-2-1 ring starts when that sentence ENDS; nothing moves or changes shade in the silence. Reveal on the reveal sentence: trap red, struck, "Trap" pill; right green with check; other at 50 %. **Option rows keep their height after the reveal** (or shrink with a 0.6 s eased tween, never a jump); explanation on the right (>= 30 px) + proof mini-scene below, so no half of the frame is empty.
- **Colour code (whole lesson, fixed):** Tools = amber #d98a00 (wrench, sockets, adapter); Knowledge = teal #0f7b8a (only as callback); premium = gold star badge on a dark tile; custom = amber dashed outline with a small spanner; data policy = red #c4392f gate (L05 style); connection = blue key; correct = green only. Nexo = F01-F04 blue robot. No SFX.
- **Continuity:** same Contoso IT floor, Teams door, Nexo's desk, the four IT people, and the F04 library wing (dim, background only). **No repeats:** no systems wall, no new-employee slots, no settings console, no badge gate, no workshop or engine room.
