## Global rules for this lesson (F06 rules + F06 review fixes, all mandatory)
- **Lint first:** `node tools/video/lint-layout.mjs F07` must show **no BLOQUEANTE** before any full render (BORDE-CORTADO, SOLAPE, VACIO, DOBLE-EXPOSICION).
- **Margins:** every subject, label, panel and chip >= 120 px from every frame edge at 1080p, bottom edge included, in every shot.
- **Text size: ALL text >= 32 px at 1080p**, no exceptions (labels, sub-labels, UI rows, field values, reveal text, proof chips, bubbles, tags). If it does not fit, cut words or zoom; never shrink.
- **Full frames:** every resting shot fills width and height; no group leaning to one side, no empty band > 20 % (scene 0 and the close included). UI windows fill ~80 % of the frame. No empty boxes or placeholder rectangles: if a slot has nothing to show, it is not drawn.
- **Names on screen:** whenever the voice names a thing (a channel, a panel, a button, an option), its label is on screen at that moment, in its own slot.
- **Reserved slots, no covering:** every card, list or chip has a free slot reserved in the layout before it appears; nothing covers another object without a story reason (e.g. a curtain closing).
- **State matches the story:** nothing appears before it is delivered or deployed. Example: the Teams channel shows the old answer until the Publish moment; the new answer only appears in Teams after "Publish" and a new session. The audience is outside the doors until they open.
- **Contrast:** dimmed/grey items use #5b6578 text on #e7eaf1 (readable), never pale grey on white. No ghosts: a faded element is fully gone (opacity 0) before anything else uses its place.
- **Transitions:** no empty or grey frame > 0.5 s; crossfade ≤ 0.5 s, next scene's first object enters while the previous one leaves. Something happens on every sentence; the close has motion until the 3 s tail.
- **Pills:** at most 2 on screen, reserved slots (top-left / bottom-centre, >= 120 px from edges); none longer than ~15 s. Chip texts exactly as written here.
- **Exam rooms:** question card **and** all three options enter **together** at s0 ("Your turn."), dim, in full-width rows (~75 % of the width, ring at right); each option brightens on its own sentence. On-screen options drop the spoken "Or" and the "?". After "Decide before I answer." the fixer inserts **3.5 s of silence**; the 3-2-1 ring starts when that sentence ENDS; nothing moves or changes shade in the silence. Reveal on the reveal sentence: trap red, struck, "Trap" pill; right green with check; other at 50 %. Option rows shrink with an eased tween **>= 0.5 s**; the explanation (right) and the proof panels (below) enter **after** the rows finish shrinking; proof panels sized to their content and filled (no empty half).
- **Colour code (whole lesson, fixed):** Draft / test = amber #d98a00 (rehearsal light, "DRAFT" tag); Published / live = green #1e7f4f only for the live version and correct answers; old version = grey-blue #5a6b8c with an "old" tag; activity map = purple #8a5cd6 path; authentication = blue #2f5bd3 badge; no authentication = red #c4392f open gate. Nexo = F01-F06 blue robot. No SFX.
- **Continuity:** same Contoso people, Nexo robot, Teams door, the maker. **No repeats:** no business park, no moving boxes, no adapter board, no library wing, no settings console.

## 4 EXAM: the old answer (pause s7, reveal s8) — exam room
- Q (visible from s0): "The team fixed a wrong answer in a topic and saved it. A day later, employees in Teams still get the old answer, even in brand new chats. What is missing?"

- Proof (enters after the rows shrink, fills the lower half): left panel "Saved only": stage card amber "DRAFT", Teams door card grey "old", a clock spinning with no change; right panel "Published": lever down, Teams door card green "new", tick.
