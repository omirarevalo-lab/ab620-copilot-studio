## Global rules for this lesson (L01 rules + F08 review fixes, all mandatory)
- **Lint first:** `node tools/video/lint-layout.mjs L03 --png` must show **no BLOQUEANTE** before any full render (BORDE-CORTADO, SOLAPE, VACIO, DOBLE-EXPOSICION). Review frames after the LAST edit.
- **Margins:** every subject, label, panel and chip >= 120 px from every frame edge at 1080p, bottom edge included, in every shot (pans and zooms too: never cut a sign at the edge while resting).
- **Text size: ALL text >= 32 px at 1080p**, no exceptions: labels, sub-labels, card sub-lines, edition numbers, UI rows, reveal text, proof chips. If it does not fit, cut words or zoom; never shrink.
- **Full frames from the first second:** every shot fills width and height (scene 0 and the close included); no empty band > 20 %; characters large (Valeria, Marta and Nexo >= 300 px tall in close shots). No empty placeholder boxes: a stand, slot or row is drawn only with its content, and content lights **on the word that names it**.
- **Names on screen, exact:** when the voice names a channel, connector, button or idea, its label (exact words of this storyboard) is on screen in its own slot.
- **Reserved slots, no covering:** cards and chips enter only in slots reserved for them; nothing covers another object; moving objects (manuals, the delivery van) follow a free path.
- **No ghosts, no double exposures:** in any crossfade or swap on the same spot (old edition → new edition, draft → printed), the old element reaches opacity 0 **before** the new one starts (sequential, ≤ 0.25 s each), never overlapped.
- **State matches the story;** dimmed items #5b6578 on #e7eaf1. **UI windows** fill ~80 % of the frame with no empty half (rows sized to content). **Dialogs opaque** from their first frame.
- **Transitions:** no empty or grey frame > 0.5 s. Something happens on every sentence; the close moves until the 3 s tail.
- **Pills:** at most 2 on screen, reserved slots (top-left / bottom-centre); none longer than ~15 s. Chip texts exactly as written here.
- **Exam rooms:** question card **and** all three options enter **together** at s0 ("Your turn."), dim, full-width rows (~75 % width, ring at right); each option brightens on its own sentence (s4, s5, s6). On-screen options drop the spoken "Or" and the "?". After "Decide before I answer." the fixer inserts **3.5 s of silence**; the 3-2-1 ring starts when that sentence ENDS; nothing moves in the silence. **Reveal exactly at `S(i, 8)`**. Trap red, struck, "Trap" pill; right green with check; other at 50 %. Option rows shrink with an eased tween **>= 0.8 s**; explanation (right) and proof panels (below) enter **after** the rows finish; proof panels sized to their content and filled.
- **Colour code (whole lesson, fixed, reused in exams and close):** Draft = grey dashed paper #5b6578 with a pencil; **old edition = amber #d98a00 manual "Ed. 1"**; **new edition = blue #2f5bd3 manual "Ed. 2"**; Publish press = navy #1f2a44; Teams and Microsoft 365 stand = purple #8a5cd6; Direct Line family (Demo website, Custom website, Mobile app) = teal #0f7b8a, all three under one teal awning; WhatsApp and Facebook stands = plain grey-blue with text labels only (no logos); session = an hourglass/clock chip; data-policy connector = a labelled switch plug per stand, **Blocked = dark slate #3b4252 padlock** (never red). Correct = green #1e7f4f only; trap = red #c4392f only. Nexo = blue robot; Valeria = F08/L01 developer (purple top); **Marta = new character, Contoso sales (coral #e07a5f top, phone/laptop)**; **Admin = new character, Contoso administrator (grey jacket, key-card badge)**. No SFX. No brand logos anywhere.
- **Continuity:** Valeria's lab desk from L01. **No repeats:** no theatre (F08 used theatre doors for channels), no river of bridges, no business park, no service city, no trail map, no library wing.

- Q (visible from s0): "Valeria saved a change, and it works in the test pane. But users on Teams and on the website don't see it, even in new conversations. What is missing?"
