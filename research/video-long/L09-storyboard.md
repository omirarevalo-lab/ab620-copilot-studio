## Global rules for this lesson (L03/L07/L08 rules, all mandatory)
- **Lint first:** `node tools/video/lint-layout.mjs L09 --png` must show **no BLOQUEANTE** before any full render (BORDE-CORTADO, SOLAPE, VACIO, DOBLE-EXPOSICION). Review frames after the LAST edit.
- **Margins:** every subject, label, panel and chip >= 120 px from every frame edge at 1080p, bottom edge included, in every shot (pans and zooms too).
- **Text size: ALL text >= 32 px at 1080p**, no exceptions (chat bubbles, button labels, template lines, UI rows, reveal text, proof chips). Long option names ("Write the response with generative AI") get their own full-width row; never shrink.
- **Full frames, every shot (L08 lesson):** every shot fills width and height; no empty band > 20 %; no half-empty planes (if a set has a big empty area, zoom in or put the named object there). Characters large (Valeria, the user, the service manager, Nexo >= 300 px tall in close shots).
- **No empty boxes, ever (L08 lesson):** a panel, board, card or window appears **with its content** on the word that names it. Never draw a frame first and fill it later; never leave a white board behind a character.
- **No world-edge bands (L08 lesson):** the world floor/backdrop must extend past every frame edge in every camera position; no coloured ground plate or label strip (e.g. "Development") that belongs to another lesson. Scale the world (>= 1.06) rather than show its edge.
- **Names on screen, exact:** when the voice names a node, option or setting, its label is on screen in its own slot, exact capitals: "Message node", "Question node", "Quick reply", "Message variations", "multiple choice", "After running", "Don't respond", "Write the response with generative AI", "Send specific response", "Send an adaptive card", "Power Fx", "Add node".
- **Reserved slots, no covering;** moving objects (buttons sliding under a bubble, the template card) follow a free path.
- **No ghosts, no double exposures:** in any swap on the same spot (Monday bubble → Tuesday bubble, typed text → picked option, variation 1 → 2 → 3), the old element reaches opacity 0 **before** the new one starts (sequential, ≤ 0.25 s each).
- **State matches the story;** dimmed items #5b6578 on #e7eaf1. **UI windows** ~80 % of the frame, rows sized to content. **Dialogs and panels opaque** from their first frame.
- **Transitions:** no empty or grey frame > 0.5 s; cut straight into exam rooms (no washed fade). Something happens on every sentence; the close moves until the 3 s tail.
- **Pills:** at most 2 on screen, reserved slots (top-left / bottom-centre); none longer than ~15 s.
- **Exam rooms:** question card **and** all three options enter **together** at s0 ("Your turn."), dim, full-width rows (~75 % width, ring at right); each option brightens on its own sentence (s4, s5, s6). On-screen options drop the spoken "Or" and the "?". After "Decide before I answer." the fixer inserts **3.5 s of silence**; the 3-2-1 ring starts when that sentence ENDS; nothing moves in the silence. **Reveal exactly at `S(i, 8)`**. Trap red, struck, "Trap" pill; right green with check; other at 50 %. Rows shrink with an eased tween **>= 0.8 s**; explanation (right) and proof panels (below) enter **after** the rows finish, **each proof panel complete from its first frame** (all its content drawn at once, sized to content, no empty half).
- **Colour code (whole lesson, fixed, reused in exams and close):** **Handwritten note = cream paper #f4ead2 with pen strokes** (free wording, generative AI); **Form with checkboxes = white card with a navy #1f2a44 border and ticked boxes** (multiple choice Question); **Quick replies = light-blue pill buttons #cfe0ff under a bubble** (suggest); **Message variations = three stacked speech bubbles in violet #7b5cd6**, one lit at a time (random wording); **Send specific response = a gold #b8860b stencil/template card** with two variable slots "{number}" "{status}"; **adaptive card = a teal #0f7b8a card with a small input**; **tool = a navy toolbox "Ticket lookup"**.
