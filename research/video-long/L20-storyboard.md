## Fact notes (checked)
- Human in the loop = a person takes part in the automatic process, like a boss who signs before something is sent; a flow can ask for an approval or a piece of data and wait for the answer before moving on — theory L20 intro; glossary "Human in the loop".
- Run a multistage approval, Human review connector, preview; combines manual stages, AI stages and conditions; only in agent flows; responses through the Approvals app in Teams, Outlook or the Power Automate portal — theory "Multistage approvals (preview)"; glossary "Multistage approval".
- Manual stage: approval type First to respond or Everyone must approve, title, assigned to, details; the same approver cannot be assigned to several manual stages — theory "Manual and AI stages"; exam tips; glossary "First to respond". "Everyone must approve needs every approver to say yes" is the plain reading of the type name only.
- AI stage: instructions and inputs (documents, images, text, knowledge), returns Approve or Reject with a rationale; choose the model and select Test — theory "Manual and AI stages"; step 5.
- Conditions route, approve, reject or skip stages based on criteria; without conditions approved moves to the next stage and rejected ends the process; left side only an input — theory "Conditions".
- Request for information: pauses the flow, asks one or more people through Outlook (same tenant), first response wins, input types text, yes/no, email, number, date, outputs as dynamic content — theory "Request for information"; glossary.
- Contoso case: AI stage checks the amount is within budget and returns Approve or Reject with its rationale; a condition sends amounts above a certain threshold to a manual management stage with First to respond, otherwise it ends approved; missing cost center via Request for information through Outlook — theory L20 example.
